Vocabulary for Speaking

Ten units from English Vocabulary in Use (Pre-intermediate & Intermediate), chosen for the CEFR Multilevel and IELTS speaking tests. Learn each word, see it in a real text, then say it in an exam answer.

How this site helps you

  1. Learn the word. A simple meaning and an example sentence. Say each word out loud. Hide the meanings to test yourself.
  2. Read it in context. A short text with the new words highlighted. Tap any highlighted word to check its meaning.
  3. Use it in speaking. A real exam-style question, a model answer that uses the words, sentence starters, and a timer so you can answer out loud.
  4. Check yourself. Common mistakes to avoid and a three-question quiz. Then mark the unit as done.

Study one unit a day and you will finish in ten days. Always say the model answer out loud at least once.
